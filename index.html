Why Water & Rotation Matter

Planting high-water crops like paddy during water deficits leads to severe crop failure. Meanwhile, rotating a pulse crop fixes up to 30–50 kg atmospheric nitrogen per hectare into the soil.

How AgriSmart AI Works

Four transparent, rule-driven steps simulating intelligent agronomic decision making.

1

Enter Farm Details

Input your soil texture, pH, NPK nutrients, climate, water availability, and previous crop history.

2

Agronomic Multi-Factor Matching

The engine compares farm inputs against curated agricultural crop benchmarks for temperature, rainfall, and soil limits.

3

Normalized Suitability Scoring

Weights for soil (22%), water (18%), season (20%), pH (15%), climate (15%), and rotation (10%) compute a score from 0–100.

4

Review Recommendations & Guidance

Receive the top 3 ranked crops accompanied by transparent explanations, water warnings, and soil tips.

📋

No Hidden "Black-Box" AI

AgriSmart AI uses documented agronomic rules rather than opaque generative AI. You can review all matching criteria, penalty conditions, and rotation logic transparently.

About The Project

Empowering Farmers with Accessible Technology

AgriSmart AI was conceived as a lightweight, beginner-friendly decision support system designed specifically for Indian agriculture. Smallholder farmers often face crop failures due to improper crop selection, declining groundwater, and lack of rotation awareness.

By operating 100% locally in the browser with zero cloud dependencies, API keys, or paid services, AgriSmart AI guarantees privacy, zero recurring cost, and instant performance even in low-bandwidth rural environments.

✓
Better-Informed Selection: Aligns crops with genuine soil chemistry and water limits.
✓
Resource Conservation: Prevents water-intensive crop planting during critical groundwater stress.
✓
Dual Language Support: Instant toggle between English and Telugu (తెలుగు) for accessibility.
